Tournament Recovery Mode

Hydration Education Hub

  • Pre-practice: Start hydrated; pale-yellow urine is a good sign.
  • During practice: Sip regularly, not all at once.
  • Electrolytes: Useful in long/hot sessions or high sweat loss.

Urine Color Quick Guide

Pale yellow: hydrated | Dark yellow: drink more | Amber: dehydration risk.

Nutrition for Immunity

  • Carbs support training stress tolerance.
  • Protein supports repair and immune defenses.
  • Colorful produce + adequate sleep reduce burnout risk.

Coach/Parent Support Mode

Supportive monitoring only. No shaming, no punitive language.

Healthy Weight-Class Selector Principles

  • Prioritize stable performance and recovery over rapid short-term cuts.
  • Avoid targets that require repeated severe dehydration.
  • Use coach/trainer input for class decisions.

Red-Flag Alert System

Triggers include rapid weight drops, repeated skipped meals, low sleep, and low energy trends. Alerts are supportive and suggest speaking with qualified adults or professionals.
